01The idea+

Space Captains is an indirect control game in the spirit of Majesty. Every captain has a personality, a wallet and opinions. You post work and a price. They decide if it is worth the risk.

You control

What gets built, which contracts exist, how much they pay, which projects get funded, where defence zones sit.

They control

Which job to take, the route, when to fight or flee, what gear to buy, when to rest and repair.

The trade

Rich work is dangerous. Pay too little and nobody goes. Pay well and you go broke. Your job is finding the price that moves the right people.

02First ten minutes+
  1. Look around. Press F to see the whole system, then H to fly back to your station. The left panel lists planets, asteroid clusters and threats.
  2. Build a Mining Outpost. Press F1 or click its tile in the build bar, then click a spot near the station. Construction drones assemble it.
  3. Hire miners. Select the outpost and hire two or three captains. Each arrives with their own ship and a little cash.
  4. Post a mining contract. Click Cinder Cluster (iron, safe) in the left panel or on the map. Set a pay per load near the fair price shown and post it. Miners will start taking loads.
  5. Sell ore. Shuttles carry ore to the station. The Factory buys it for credits and turns it into modules that captains buy.
  6. Get protection. Raiders are out there from the start. Build a Mercenary Outpost (F2), hire mercs, and expect the first raid around minute five.

Sasa ke? Belter for "understand?". If nobody takes a contract, select it: the panel shows each captain's reason for saying no.

06Captains+

You hire captains at outposts. Each one flies their own ship, keeps their own wallet and has a personality that bends every choice they make.

Miner

MINING OUTPOST

Takes mining contracts, flies to an asteroid cluster, fills the hold and brings it back. Shuttles carry the ore on to the station.

Mercenary

MERC OUTPOST

Takes bounties and escort contracts, patrols home and defence zones, answers alarms. Groups of 2 to 6 form fleets for big targets.

Salvager

SALVAGE YARD

Works without contracts. Flies to wrecks after fights, hauls scrap to the yard, which turns it into ore and sells it to you cheap.

Lander

RAIDER DOCK

Flies a dropship down to a planet, strips what the planet has and climbs back out. Needs research first.

Personality

  • Greedy chases the best pay and rich, risky work.
  • Cautious avoids danger and asks for more to take it.
  • Bold likes a fight and goes further out.
  • Lazy wants short trips and takes breaks.

Captains spend their own money on modules and repairs, gain reputation and rank, and pick up traits over time. Select a captain to see their attributes, gear and decision log.

Fair pay

The post form shows a fair price for that spot. Below it, captains mostly refuse. Well above it, even cautious ones go. Danger and distance raise the fair price.

Cancelling a bounty

Nobody took it: you get 100% back. Someone took it: you get 50% back and the takers split the rest. If the target escapes you get it all back.

A contract you cannot afford to pay pauses and shows Paused, station can't pay. Captains will not take it until your credits recover.

  • The Factory panel shows what it is making, what captains want, and its prices. Auto-sell keeps a minimum of each ore and sells the rest.

10Research+

You start with one Mk I module per slot. Everything else is researched at the Science Lab.

  1. Hire researchers. Up to three. Each has a field (Engineering, Weapons or Planetary), a skill from 1 to 10 and a personality. Applicants change every four minutes.
  2. Fund projects. Funding reserves the credits and spends the ore. You can fund several at once.
  3. They choose. Idle researchers pick a funded project that suits their field and temperament. The lab log says why.

Who picks what

Ambitious goes for big, expensive tiers. Cautious likes defensive work and avoids weapons. Curious loves Planetary and new buildings. Methodical finishes what is started and prefers cheap tier I work.

Cancelling

Nobody started it: everything comes back. Work started: half the credits and ore come back.

Working outside their field makes a researcher slower. Skill rises after every two finished projects.

12Threats+

Scavengers

Cheap, numerous and easy prey for one merc. They steal cargo from miners.

Corsairs

Fast and hard to hit. They dodge and often slip away.

Syndicate

Heavy gunships. One merc usually retreats. Send two or more, and defend with turrets.

  • Factions arrive one at a time. Rustjaw is there from the start, and a new base shows up later as your colony grows. A warning comes about a minute before.
  • Hit a base and it calls its raiders home to defend it and launches ships faster for a while.
  • Raids start around minute five with one raider and grow bigger and more frequent. The feed warns you 30 seconds before one launches and names the target.
  • Big bases need fleets. The bounty panel tells you how many mercs a target needs and how many are free.
13Defence+

Defence zones

Up to three, radius 0.8 to 4 km. Mercs on patrol roam inside them and engage raiders that come in. Give a zone a kill bounty and a budget, and greedy mercs will love it. Turret kills pay nothing.

Colony alarm

When an outpost, the station, a shuttle or the shield is hit, free mercs within 5 km come to help. The feed says who answered.

Turrets and shield

Two turrets and a shield hold off small raids on their own. Turrets drive raiders off; mercs finish them. Against the Syndicate you need both.

Damage

Outposts have 1600 hull and the station 5000. At zero a structure is disabled, never destroyed, and repairs itself for about 10 credits per 100 hull. A shot-down shuttle loses its cargo.

14Saving+

The game saves every three minutes of play, when you go back to the menu, and when you quit. Each game has its own slot. Continue loads the newest one. If a save is damaged the menu offers its backup.

15Tips+
  • Read refusals. A captain's decision log shows the exact numbers behind every yes and no.
  • Start on the safe iron cluster and move outward once you have mercs.
  • Clusters run out as they are mined. Watch the feed for a survey signal: a new one turns up a few minutes later.
  • Raise pay a little rather than a lot. Slightly above fair usually gets the job done.
  • Keep some credits free. Contracts pause when the station cannot pay.
  • Turn down auto-sell for goods you want to refine or build with.
  • Put a defence zone over your outposts early, before the raids grow.
